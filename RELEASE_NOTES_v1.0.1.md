# 🌊 Oceanic v1.0.1: Smoother, smarter, more *you*

Hey again 👋

Thank you so much for trying Oceanic! This update is packed: a brand-new Settings, easier sharing, a better study timer, and loads of small fixes that make every day feel smoother.

---

## ✨ What's new

### ⚙️ All-new Settings
- A fresh, cleaner Settings page with sections on the left and a **search bar**, so you can find any setting in seconds.
- **Your profile:** add a profile picture, an emoji, or a coloured initial.
- **Pick your accent colour** and make Oceanic feel like home.
- **Change the text size** in your notes.
- **Start your week on Sunday or Monday.** Your calendar follows.
- Choose your Home layout and style, toolbar style, and whether the minimap shows in mind maps.
- **Open Oceanic when your computer starts**, or **keep it in the system tray** when you close it.
- Check for updates any time with **Check now**.
- A new **Keyboard shortcuts** page to help you work faster.

### 📦 Share your work with Oceanic files
- Export any page as an **Oceanic file (.ocean)**. It keeps *everything*: subpages, images and properties. Your friend gets your notes exactly as you made them.
- Save mind maps as **.omap** files and import them back in.
- **Double-click** a `.ocean` or `.omap` file on your computer and it opens straight in Oceanic.

### 🚀 Jump between apps faster
- The new **More apps** menu takes you straight back to where you left off in Notes, Docs, Mindmap or Sketch.
- A quick, smooth loading screen when you switch apps. You can turn it off in Settings if you prefer.
- **Open now:** each app's Home shows the files you still have open, so you can hop right back in.
- The Oceanic logo now looks right on both light and dark backgrounds.

### ⏱️ Better study timer
- Set focus and break times in **minutes and seconds**.
- A gentle **chime** plays when your session ends. You can switch it off in Settings.

### 🔗 Smarter quick links
- Quick links on Study Home now show the **website's icon**. You can also pick an emoji or a colour instead.
- **Edit** your links, and right-click to **Open**, **Edit** or **Remove** them.

### 📄 Docs and ✏️ Sketch
- Fresh, cleaner cards on the Docs and Sketch home screens.
- Click anywhere on the page in Docs, even the margins, to start typing, just like in Word.
- **Delete** files right from Docs and Sketch. They go to your Recycle Bin, so you can still get them back.
- Right-click a sketch to **Open** it, **Show it in File Explorer**, or delete it.

### 🛟 No more accidental deletes
- Oceanic now **asks before deleting** anything. Prefer no pop-ups? Turn this off per app in **Settings → Deleting**.

### 💬 Helpful tooltips
- Hover over any button to see a short tip explaining what it does.

---

## 🐛 Bug fixes
- Imported notes now show their content straight away instead of opening blank.
- If some files can't be imported, Oceanic now tells you which ones.
- New text boxes in Sketch are ready to type in straight away.
- The loading screen is shorter and smoother.
- Lots of small layout and polish fixes across the app.
