# 🌊 Oceanic v1.0.0: Hello, world!

Hey there 👋

Oceanic is finally here! We built it because studying shouldn't mean juggling five apps, a messy notebook and a dozen browser tabs. Your notes, docs, sketches, mind maps and deadlines now live in one calm place.

This is our very first release. Here's what you get.

---

## 📝 Take notes your way
Just start typing. Add headings, bullet lists, numbered lists, to-dos, toggles, quotes and code blocks whenever you need them. You can drag blocks around to reorder your thoughts, and **Turn into** changes any block into a different kind.

Pages can also hold **tables**, **audio** clips and **mind maps**, so your lecture recording can sit right next to the notes you took during it.

## 🎒 Organised by subject, the way school is
Maths, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, History, Geography, Economics, Computing, Art, Music, Languages… Give each subject its own home, add **tags**, and give your pages emoji icons so you can spot them instantly.

## 🔗 Connect the dots
Link your pages together. **Backlinks** show you every page that mentions the one you're on, and **Peek** lets you look at a linked page without losing your place. Long notes get an **outline** so you can jump straight to the right section.

## 👀 See your work differently
- **Document view** for reading and writing
- **Calendar view** for deadlines, exams and study sessions
- **Spatial canvas** to spread your ideas out like sticky notes on a desk

## 🧠 Mind maps
Got a topic that just won't click? Map it out. Every map you make is kept in your mind map gallery.

## ✏️ Oceanic Sketch
A space for doodles, diagrams, graphs and the "let me just draw it" moments.

## 📄 Oceanic Docs
A clean writing space for essays, reports and assignments.

## 🔍 Find anything, fast
Search through everything you've written in seconds, so you're not hunting through folders the night before an exam.

## 📤 Share and submit
Export any page as a **PDF**, **Markdown** or **HTML** file. Handy for submitting work or sending notes to a friend.

## 💾 Your notes stay yours
Everything is saved in a folder you choose on your own computer. Deleted something by accident? It goes to the **Trash** first, so you can bring it back.

## 🎨 Make it feel like home
Choose your theme, fonts and appearance in Settings. Oceanic will also let you know when a new update is ready.

---

## 💙 One last thing
This is just the beginning. If something feels off, or you've got an idea that would make studying easier, we'd genuinely love to hear it.

Now go ace those exams. 📚✨
